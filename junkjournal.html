Autumnal Inspiration

October 2026 Vision Board

There is something special that comes in air with autumn wind - something that makes me want to slow down and take notice of all the little things around me. To enjoy cozy evenings, both out and in. The changing leaves, the warm fires and drinks, the seasonal baking, and all the tiny traditions that make this time of year feel special.
For this month's Junk Journal, I wanted to create a snapshot of what I want my autumn to feel like.

For me, an autumnal vision board makes me want to try new things and find new traditions each season. Your board can be whatever you want it to be - what does Auumn feel lik to you?

My favorite part about creating seasonal or monthly vision boards - you get to collect and use the things that truly inspire you, rather than just using whatever you can find.

What You'll Need

  • Scrap paper
  • Sprocket mini printer or printed photos
  • Washi tape
  • Markers
  • Optional: Glue stick

Thankfully, there's no wrong or right way to make a vision board! I used my Sprocket mini printer to print inspiration pictures on sticker paper, which I put directly into my journal, along with smaller stickers and letters. You can paste images in as they are, or cut out shapes from the images. Many use this kind of journaling to utilize saved up scraps or even to refer back to when planning out future weeks.
