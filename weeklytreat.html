🕒 Time Requirements

  • Prep Time: 15 mins
  • Cook Time: 25 mins
  • Total Time: 40 mins

This Week's Treat: Homemade Cinnamon Rolls

📋 Ingredients

  • 2 cups All-Purpose Flour
  • 2 tbsp Granulated Sugar
  • 1 packet Instant Yeast
  • 1/2 cup Warm Milk
  • 2 tbsp Melted Butter
  • 1 tbsp Ground Cinnamon & Brown Sugar (for filling)

👩‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Prep the dough: Mix flour, sugar, and yeast in a large bowl. Pour in warm milk and melted butter, mixing until a soft dough forms.
  2. Roll & Fill: Roll out the dough into a rectangle on a floured surface. Brush with extra butter and sprinkle generously with brown sugar and cinnamon.
  3. Cut & Bake: Roll tightly into a log, slice into 1-inch rounds, and place in a greased baking dish. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 25 minutes until golden brown.
